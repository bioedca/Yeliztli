EMBL-EBI terms of use

Last revised:      14th October 2026

General

  1. EMBL’s European Bioinformatics Institute (EMBL-EBI) promotes open science through its mission to provide freely available Data Resources and Tools (which includes online services, databases and software) to the largest possible community. Where EMBL-EBI presents scientific data generated by others, EMBL-EBI imposes no additional restriction on the use of the contributed data than those provided by the data owner, unless otherwise specified in these Terms of Use.
  2. EMBL-EBI expects attribution (e.g. in publications, services or products) for the use of any of its Data Resources and Tools in accordance with good scientific practice. The expected attribution will be indicated on the relevant web page of the resource.
  3. Any feedback provided to EMBL-EBI on its Data Resources and Tools shall be considered as non-confidential.
  4. All scientific data will be made available by a time and release mechanism consistent with the data type (e.g. human data where access needs to be reviewed by a Data Access Committee, pre-publication embargoed for a specific time period). 
  5. EMBL-EBI processes personal data according to its internal policy, which safeguards data for individuals. EMBL-EBI also reserves the right to collect data through cookies and registration. Personal data will be disclosed only in accordance with the applicable privacy notice for the relevant data resource or tool (see also Privacy section below). 
  6. EMBL-EBI may make information about the total usage of particular Data Resources and Tools, or data available, to the public and third party organisations who supply or fund the Data Resources and Tools without details of any individual’s use. Such information is in the form of aggregated statistics and does not identify any individual.
  7. While EMBL-EBI will retain its commitment to Open Science, EMBL-EBI reserves the right to update these Terms of Use at any time. When alterations are inevitable, EMBL-EBI will attempt to give reasonable notice of any changes by placing a notice on its website, but users may wish to check each time they use the website. The date of the most recent revision will appear on this, the ‘EMBL-EBI Terms of Use’ page. If the user does not agree to these changes, please do not continue to use EMBL-EBI Data Resources and Tools. EMBL-EBI will also make available on request an archived copy of the previous Terms of Use for comparison.
  8. EMBL-EBI shall not be liable to the user or third parties claiming rights through the user for any losses or damages. 
  9. For some Data Resources and Tools, additional specific terms and conditions and/or license agreements may apply, which in case of any conflict of inconsistency, will prevail over these Terms of Use.

Data 

  1. The Data Resources and Tools hosted by EMBL-EBI are generated in part from data contributed by the community who remain the data owners.
  2. When the user contributes scientific data to a database through EMBL-EBI website or other submission tools this information will be released at a time and in a manner consistent with the scientific data and EMBL-EBI may store it permanently.
  3. EMBL-EBI itself places no additional restrictions on the use or redistribution of the data available via its Data Resources and Tools other than those provided by the original data owners, unless otherwise specified in these Terms of Use.
  4. The original data may be subject to rights claimed by third parties, including but not limited to, patent, copyright, other intellectual property rights and Access and Benefit Sharing. For the specific case of the EGA database and human data consented for biomedical research, these rights may be formalised in Data Access Agreements. For information on how to manage cookies in different web browsers and a full list of EMBL-EBI and 3rd party cookies — see our cookies page.
